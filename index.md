# Privacy Policy for civik

**Last updated:** August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how civik ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information, all of which are linked to your account identity:

- **Email Address** — collected when you create an account, used for authentication and login.
- **User ID** — an internal identifier generated when you create an account, used to associate your data and activity within the App.
- **Precise Location** — collected when you use the App's map features, used to show relevant content and functionality based on where you are.
- **Photos** — collected when you upload images within the App, used to display and store your content.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above solely to operate and provide the core functionality of the App, including:

- Creating and securing your account
- Powering the map and location-based features
- Storing and displaying photos you choose to upload
- Providing AI-assisted features within the App

We do **not** use your data for advertising, and we do **not** track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Third-Party Services

civik relies on the following third-party service providers to operate:

- **Supabase** — used for account authentication and data storage. Supabase stores your data on our behalf as our backend infrastructure provider.
- **Groq** — used to power AI-assisted features within the App. Content you submit to these features may be processed by Groq's API in order to generate a response.

These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for their own independent purposes.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

## Your Choices

You may:
- Update or delete your account information within the App
- Disable location permissions at any time through your device settings (note: this may limit map-related functionality)
- Contact us to request deletion of your account and associated data

## Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide you the App's functionality. You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

## Children's Privacy

civik is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above.
